# Third-Party Integrations and Safety

## OpenAI

- API use is verified in source.
- Account authorization and model evidence must be archived without exposing the API key.
- The application must comply with applicable OpenAI terms and usage policies.

## Google Drive and OAuth

- Drive is used as governed operational memory and document storage.
- The project must request minimum necessary scopes, disclose data use, protect client credentials, and support revocation.
- Verification in progress must not be described as approval.

## Cloudflare

- Workers and KV provide hosting, APIs, cache, memory, and records.
- The final evidence should identify the account agreement and bindings without publishing secret values.

## CNJ DataJud

- The connector is read-only and limited to public process metadata.
- DataJud does not provide case-file documents.
- National party-name or CPF search is not available through the public connector.
- The official DataJud terms require legal review before public or commercial use of derived information.
- No real case data should appear in the submission video.

## Safety boundaries

- fictional data only;
- no real legal records or confidential uploads;
- no invented process, party, source, author, page, deadline, or URL;
- no generative fallback for structured party search;
- no transactional PDPJ, MNI, filing, service acknowledgment, or court act;
- no production credentials in the repository or package;
- human review before real legal use.

## Asset rights

Every submitted image, logo, font, icon, voice, music track, and document must have a recorded author or license. The final video should use original narration and no music unless rights are documented.
