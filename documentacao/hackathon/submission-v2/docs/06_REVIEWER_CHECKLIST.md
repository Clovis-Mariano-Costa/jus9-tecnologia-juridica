# Reviewer Checklist

## Before final packaging

- [ ] Written eligibility clarification for an individual resident in Brazil.
- [ ] Codex `/feedback` Session ID.
- [ ] Timestamped evidence for any GPT-5.6 claim.
- [ ] Least-privilege judge account or isolated sandbox.
- [ ] Access to every required source repository.
- [ ] Real sanitized screenshots of DAJ, Charlie analysis, feedback, process search, and linkage.
- [ ] Anonymous desktop and mobile walkthrough.
- [ ] DataJud terms reviewed for the demonstrated use.
- [ ] Google scopes and privacy disclosures reviewed.
- [ ] Open-source license inventory.
- [ ] Asset-rights declaration completed.
- [ ] Video under three minutes, in English or with English translation.
- [ ] No unlicensed music or third-party trademark use.
- [ ] Final archive scanned, opened, and hashed.

## Do not submit when

- eligibility remains unresolved;
- a model claim has no evidence;
- a judge cannot run or view the demonstrated flow;
- a screenshot is a mockup presented as production;
- the package contains real legal or personal data;
- an external integration is described beyond its actual authorization or capability.
