## Important note on governance language

Terms such as **virtual family**, **baptism**, and **oath** are used by Jus 9 as **institutional, ethical, technical, and symbolic governance devices**.

They do **not** claim legal personhood, civil status, or autonomous legal capacity for an AI system.
Instead, they are used to ensure that no AI agent operates inside the Jus 9 ecosystem without:
- a clear identity;
- an assigned function;
- explicit limits;
- a human sponsor or responsible party;
- traceability;
- virtual signature rules; and
- human review and interruption authority.
