# 01 — Project Overview

**Jus 9 Tecnologia Jurídica** is being developed as a modular legal technology ecosystem.
Its mission is to expand access to justice, automate repetitive legal work, and strengthen responsible governance in the use of AI.

At the current stage, the project is focused on the development and validation of an MVP supported by OpenAI models and web technologies.

## Core direction
- legal AI with human supervision;
- document and workflow support;
- modular ecosystem design;
- responsible automation;
- privacy, traceability, and access control;
- future expansion toward integrations and specialized agents.

## Why it matters
The legal sector often struggles with fragmented information, repetitive tasks, access barriers, and low technological integration.
Jus 9 aims to address these issues with a more accessible, structured, and accountable approach.
