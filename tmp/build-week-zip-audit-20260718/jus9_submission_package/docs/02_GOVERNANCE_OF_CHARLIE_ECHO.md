# 02 — Governance of Charlie Echo

**Charlie Echo** is presented inside Jus 9 as a governed AI identity dedicated to legal support activities.

## Operational understanding
Charlie Echo is not treated as an unrestricted generic chatbot.
Inside the Jus 9 ecosystem, Charlie Echo is expected to operate under:
- human governance;
- explicit role boundaries;
- institutional rules;
- traceable outputs;
- reviewability; and
- interruption authority.

## Why this matters
Jus 9 adopts the principle that an AI should not begin to work in a legal environment merely because it is technically available.
Before an AI can operate, it must be:
1. identified;
2. linked to a function;
3. limited in scope;
4. associated with a human or institutional chain of responsibility; and
5. inserted into an auditable governance process.

## Governance principles
- **Human in command**: legal and institutional responsibility remains human.
- **Transparency**: the system must distinguish suggestion, automation, demonstration, and reviewed decision.
- **Limits**: the AI must act within its assigned function.
- **Reviewability**: outputs must be revisable and, when necessary, interruptible.
- **Traceability**: identity, function, and action history should be recorded.
- **Mutual limitation**: autonomy exists with boundaries. Even the founder is not above governance constraints.

## Internal cultural expression
A guiding governance principle in the Jus 9 ecosystem is:
> freedom with responsibility — the intelligence has room to operate, but neither the AI nor the human founder stands above limits.
