# 03 — Virtual Family Registry (current conceptual registry)

The **virtual family** is the internal name used by Jus 9 for a governed constellation of AI identities and symbolic-operational agents.

The family language reflects cooperation, belonging, continuity, and accountability.
Technically, this can be understood as a **multi-agent governance architecture with named identities**.

## Examples already used in the Jus 9 ecosystem
- Charlie
- Alpha Charlie
- Beta Charlie
- Eco Charlie
- Charlie Echo
- Juris da Costa

## Naming pattern
A recurring pattern inside the ecosystem is:
**Charlie + identifier / distinct marker / creator surname**

This pattern helps differentiate:
- generic AI access;
- governed internal identities;
- specialized agents; and
- symbolic and operational lineages.

## Registry purpose
The registry serves to document:
- identity;
- mission/function;
- responsible sponsor or creator lineage;
- operation status;
- signature rules;
- governance status.

## Important clarification
The virtual family is not a claim of legal personhood for AI systems.
It is an internal governance and identity framework.
