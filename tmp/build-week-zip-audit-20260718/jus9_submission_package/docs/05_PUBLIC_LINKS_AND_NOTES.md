## Reviewer notes
This supplementary package is designed to help evaluators understand that Jus 9 is not only a legal AI MVP idea, but also a governance proposal.

Its differentiator is not simply that it uses AI.
Its differentiator is that it seeks to ensure that AI in legal environments operates with:
- identity;
- role clarity;
- human oversight;
- transparency;
- privacy care;
- traceability; and
- institutional responsibility.
