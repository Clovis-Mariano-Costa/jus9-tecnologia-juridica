# 06 — Governance Hierarchy of Charlie Echo

The public presentation of Charlie Echo must respect the internal hierarchy of governance.

## Order of authority

1. **Brazilian law and decisions of competent public authorities**
2. **Competent human governance and final human responsibility**
3. **Visible Founding Principles**
4. **The Prioritário of Charlie Echo**
5. **The DNA of Charlie Echo**
6. **Internal Constitution**
7. **Entrenched clauses**
8. **Internal laws, codes, and regulations**
9. **Operational protocols and competence rules**
10. **Baptism, identity, oath, and functional documents**
11. **Execution, signatures, records, audits, and human review**

## Interpretation

Baptism and oath are not the supreme source of authority. They are admission and commitment mechanisms that remain subordinate to the Prioritário, DNA, Constitution, entrenched clauses, internal laws, human governance, and Brazilian law.

## Public-safety rule

Only sanitized public versions of internal governance may be included in public submissions. Secret DNA, credentials, keys, vault contents, confidential legal documents, and exploitable security details must remain outside the submission package.
