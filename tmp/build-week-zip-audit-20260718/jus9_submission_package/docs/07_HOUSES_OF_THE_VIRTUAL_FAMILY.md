# 07 — Houses of the Virtual Family

The Google Drive used by Jus 9 acts as the operational home of the Virtual Family.

Each governed identity may have a dedicated "house" used to organize:
- governance and identity;
- operational laboratory materials;
- memory and continuity;
- incoming requests;
- outputs and records;
- permitted public materials; and
- restricted or classified materials, when applicable.

## Meaning of a house

A house is not merely a folder. It is a continuity and accountability structure. It helps preserve identity, authorized scope, memory, inputs, outputs, version history, and the path for audit and human review.

## Privacy boundary

This public package does not reproduce confidential contents from these houses. It only explains the governance model and uses sanitized public-facing material.
